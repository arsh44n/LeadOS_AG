Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers regarding Meta API pricing, compliance, integration capabilities, and deployment timelines.

How is the official WhatsApp Business API different from the WhatsApp Business App?
The free phone app is limited to 1 device, requires manual typing, has strict broadcast caps (256 contacts who must save your number), and cannot connect to your CRM. The WhatsApp Business API (WABA) allows unlimited concurrent agents, enterprise-level throughput, verified green checkmark badges, AI chatbot automation, and programmatic CRM integration. LeadOS handles 100% of the API provisioning and technical setup.
How are Meta's conversation fees billed?
Meta charges per 24-hour conversation window based on the message category (Marketing, Utility, Authentication, or Service). Service conversations (initiated by a customer message) are significantly cheaper or free within Meta's monthly tier allowances. LeadOS optimizes your message templates to ensure you get the highest conversion rate at the lowest possible Meta messaging cost.
Can we keep human agents in the loop when needed?
Yes, absolutely. We design seamless Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) workflows. When a prospect requests a human, triggers an escalation keyword, or indicates high deal intent, the bot instantly pauses, pings your sales team in Slack or Microsoft Teams, and passes the entire conversation transcript over with zero friction.
Which CRMs and tools do you support out of the box?
We integrate natively with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo, and Shopify. For custom tech stacks, we build webhook-based pipelines on Make.com, n8n, Zapier, or custom AWS/GCP serverless functions to sync with PostgreSQL, MySQL, and proprietary internal APIs.
How fast can we go live?
Our standard onboarding and architecture deployment takes 7 to 14 business days. This includes Meta Business Manager verification, WABA phone number provisioning, AI prompt engineering, CRM field mapping, and sandbox load testing before official traffic launch.
